Nitro Racers

UFD Studios  ·  Junior Game Developer
Android Dec 2024 – Jan 2025

A 3D arcade racing title built on the Burnout Drift Multiplayer framework — drift-focused handling, AI opponents, garage customization, and online leaderboards, polished into a complete shippable game.

Get in Touch

Overview

Nitro Racers is a 3D arcade racer where I led vehicle handling, drift feel, UI and meta systems, and overall game polish. Starting from the Burnout Drift Multiplayer framework, I tuned the driving model to deliver responsive, satisfying drift mechanics and layered in the progression systems that turn a racing prototype into a full game.

AI opponents were implemented using Omni Vehicle AI to provide competitive single-player races, while a modular garage let players unlock and customize cars. Online leaderboards added a competitive long-tail to keep players coming back.

Key Features

🏁 Drift-Focused Handling

Custom-tuned vehicle physics built for satisfying, controllable drifts.

🤖 AI Opponents

Competitive racing AI powered by Omni Vehicle AI for full single-player races.

🔧 Garage & Customization

Modular garage system to unlock, upgrade, and customize vehicles.

🏆 Online Leaderboards

Global score tracking for competitive replay value.

🎨 UI & Game Polish

Full meta UI flow, menus, and the final layer of polish across the game.

📱 Android Build

Optimized mobile build for smooth performance on a range of devices.
