The game

Survive Area 51 Arena is a wave-based survival game on Roblox. You defend against relentless waves of enemies that come in multiple types, each one pushing you to adapt how you fight and defend. It blends direct combat with base defense, so every wave is about both shooting and planning.

To hold the line, you can build fences to block and funnel incoming enemies and plant guns that fire on the waves automatically, adding a tower-defense layer on top of the core combat. As you survive longer you unlock stronger weapons, and a leaderboard tracks how far players push.

Combat meets tower defense

What makes Survive Area 51 Arena more than a shooter is the base-defense layer sitting on top of the combat. Enemies arrive in escalating waves and in multiple types, so pure aim isn't enough; you have to plan the space you're fighting in.

Players can build fences to block and funnel incoming enemies and plant guns that fire on the waves automatically, turning each round into a mix of direct combat and tower-defense strategy. Surviving longer unlocks stronger weapons, and a leaderboard tracks how far players manage to push, which gives the game its competitive edge.
