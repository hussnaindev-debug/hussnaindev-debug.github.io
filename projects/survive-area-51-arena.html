Overview

Survive Area 51 Arena is a wave-based survival experience on Roblox. Players defend a secret base against progressively harder alien invasions, unlocking stronger weapons as they push deeper into the waves. It leans into cooperative multiplayer, weapon progression, and a competitive leaderboard that ranks players by how long they last.

More detail on my role and the systems I built for this experience is coming soon.

Key Features
