The game

Rise & Roll is a WebGL ball-rolling game built in Unity and published on itch.io, playable straight from the browser. You control a ball and swipe to set it rolling, working through levels packed with obstacles that test your timing and control.

The feel comes from its physics. The ball responds to your swipes with real momentum, so rolling, building speed, and threading past hazards all come down to reading the physics rather than memorizing inputs. Each level introduces new obstacles to keep it fresh.

Around the core loop is a shop for unlocking and equipping ball skins, and an itch.io leaderboard that turned each attempt into a race for the best score. This is one of my earlier games, so depending on itch.io's current rules the leaderboard service may no longer be live, but the system was fully built and integrated.

Momentum-driven movement

What gives Rise & Roll its feel is that the ball is genuinely physics-driven. A swipe doesn't snap it to a position; it applies real momentum and weight, so the ball builds speed, carries it, and reacts to slopes and hazards the way a rolling object should.

That means clearing a level is about reading the physics and timing your input, not memorizing a fixed path. Each new level layers in obstacles that push on that skill. Around the core loop, a shop lets players unlock and equip ball skins, and an itch.io leaderboard turned each attempt into a race for the best score.
