Rise & Roll

UFD Studios  ·  Lead Developer
WebGL Playable in Browser

A physics-based ball-rolling game where you swipe to send the ball through level after level of obstacles, unlocking new skins along the way and chasing the top of the leaderboard.

Play on itch.io ↗ Get in Touch

Overview

Rise & Roll is a WebGL ball-rolling game built in Unity and published on itch.io, playable straight from the browser. You control a ball and swipe to set it rolling, working through a series of levels packed with obstacles that test your timing and control.

The feel of the game comes from its physics. The ball responds to your swipes with real momentum, so rolling, building speed, and threading past hazards all come down to reading the physics rather than memorizing inputs. Each level introduces new obstacles to keep the challenge fresh.

Around the core loop is a shop system that lets players unlock and equip different ball skins, and an itch.io leaderboard that turned each attempt into a race for the best score. This is one of my earlier games, so depending on itch.io's current rules the leaderboard service may no longer be live, but the system was fully built and integrated.
