Overview

Pookie Park is a single-player 2D platformer. My main contribution was level design — applying pacing, readability, and difficulty progression so that levels teach mechanics naturally and ramp challenge at a satisfying rate. Good platformer levels are invisible when they work, and that was the bar.

I also integrated the Google Mobile Ads SDK with optimized ad placements that monetize without disrupting the play experience. The game reached 50,000+ downloads on Google Play.

Key Features

🗺️ Level Design

Hand-crafted levels tuned for pacing, readability, and smooth difficulty progression.

🏃 Platformer Mechanics

Responsive 2D movement and jumping at the core of the loop.

📈 Difficulty Progression

Levels that teach mechanics early and escalate challenge naturally.

📺 Google Mobile Ads
