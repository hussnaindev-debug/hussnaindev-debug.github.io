The game

Circular Pong is an arcade reflex game built around a single ring. You control a paddle that moves around the circle, keeping a colorful ball bouncing inside it. I led development end to end, building its core systems and gameplay.

Every paddle hit scores a point, so survival and scoring are one and the same. The longer you last, the trickier it gets: obstacles appear inside the ring that you steer the ball clear of, while coins are scattered around to collect along the way.

Coins feed a full progression loop, a store for ball, paddle, and ring skins, a spin wheel for rewards, plus daily rewards, a daily ad bonus, and weekly missions, giving players a reason to return every day.

The live-ops meta layer

The arcade loop is only half of Circular Pong. The other half is the economy and retention layer built on top of it. Coins earned in play feed a store where players buy and equip skins for the ball, the paddle, and the rings, giving them goals beyond a high score.

On top of that sit the re-engagement systems: a spin wheel for in-game rewards, daily rewards, a daily ad bonus, and weekly missions that each give players a reason to come back. A Google Play leaderboard turns every run into a competition for the global high score, and DOTween drives the menus and transitions. Google Ads and in-app purchases monetize the game without breaking the flow of play.
