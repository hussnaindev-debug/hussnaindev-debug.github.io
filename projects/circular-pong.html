Overview

Circular Pong is an arcade reflex game built around a single ring. You control a paddle that moves around the circle, and your job is to keep the colorful ball bouncing inside it. I led development of the game end to end, building its core systems and gameplay.

Every time the ball collides with your paddle you score a point, so survival and scoring are one and the same. The longer you last, the trickier it gets: obstacles appear inside the ring that you have to steer the ball clear of, while coins are scattered around for you to collect along the way.

Coins feed a full progression loop. A store lets players buy and equip skins for the ball, the paddle, and the rings, and a spin wheel hands out in-game rewards. Daily rewards, a daily ad bonus, and weekly missions give players a reason to come back every day.

A Google Play leaderboard turns every run into a competition for the global high score, and DOTween drives the menus and transitions for smooth, satisfying animation throughout. On the production side, I integrated Google Ads and in-app purchases to monetize the game without breaking the flow of play.
