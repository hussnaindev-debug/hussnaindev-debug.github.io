Overview

Dig to Escape (Ghost Pirate Ship) is an escape and puzzle experience on Roblox. Players are trapped inside a sunken, haunted pirate ship and have to dig through the wreck to uncover hidden escape routes, all while avoiding the Ghost Captain. It has a competitive edge too, letting friends race each other for the fastest escape time, with more than one route to find.

More detail on my role and the systems I built for this experience is coming soon.

Key Features

⛏️ Dig to Escape

Dig through the sunken ship to uncover hidden routes out of the wreck.

👻 Avoid the Ghost Captain

Stay clear of the Ghost Captain as you search for a way out.

🗺️ Multiple Escape Routes

More than one path out of the ship to discover.

⏱️ Race for the Fastest Time

Compete with friends to escape in the shortest time.
