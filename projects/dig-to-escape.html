Overview

Dig to Escape (Ghost Pirate Ship) is a recently developed escape game on Roblox. Your pirate ship has sunk and you are trapped in a haunted wreck under the sea, and the only way out is to dig your escape. It is a compact, focused experience built around a clear goal: get out.

Two systems shape the moment-to-moment play. A stats system tracks and manages your stamina as you dig, so you have to pace your effort rather than power through. A trading system lets players exchange items with each other, adding a layer of interaction and strategy on top of the escape. Together they turn a simple dig-out into a game of resource management.
