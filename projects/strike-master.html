Strike Master: Going Pins

UFD Studios  ·  Junior Game Developer
Android iOS Oct – Nov 2024

A swipe-and-drag rolling ball game with a custom movement system that keeps the ball upright while giving players precise directional control — paired with pin physics, score multipliers, and leaderboards.

Google Play ↗ App Store ↗ Get in Touch

Overview

Strike Master is a 3D rolling-ball game shipped to both Android and iOS. The core challenge was the control scheme: I built a custom movement system that keeps the ball upright and stable while still translating swipe and drag gestures into responsive directional control — the feel that makes or breaks this kind of game.

Around that core I implemented pin behaviors and physics, score multipliers to reward skilled play, leaderboards for competition, and Yodo1 ad integration for monetization across both platforms.

Key Features

🎯 Custom Movement System

Keeps the ball upright while translating swipe/drag input into precise directional control.

🎳 Pin Physics

Believable pin behaviors and collision response for satisfying strikes.

✖️ Score Multipliers

Reward systems that push players toward higher-skill runs.

🏆 Leaderboards

Competitive global score tracking.

💵 Yodo1 Ads

Integrated Yodo1 ad network for monetization.

📱 Android & iOS

Shipped cross-platform to both Google Play and the App Store.
