The game

Steal a Viral Squishy is a multiplayer squishy-collecting game for Roblox. Each server gives every player a base around a central conveyor. Squishies travel along the conveyor; players buy them, send them to a slot in their base, and those squishies generate passive income over time. I designed and built every system in the experience.

The twist is the stealing: players can enter another player's unlocked base, carry a squishy back to their own, and defend against thieves doing the same. On top of that sit rarities, a rebirth system, mystery boxes, a full monetization framework, a first-time tutorial, and analytics, all on a server-authoritative setup so the economy and theft can't be exploited from the client.

My role

Solo Developer · All Systems

I built the game on my own, from the ground up: the multiplayer base and conveyor system, passive income, the server-authoritative stealing and base-locking, defensive tools, rarities, rebirth, mystery-box gacha, the full monetization framework, the first-time tutorial and beginner quests, the cross-platform UI, and the analytics funnel, with DataStore saving and exploit/dupe protection throughout.

What I built

The full system set, from the multiplayer core loop to monetization and analytics.

Bases & conveyor

Per-player bases around a central conveyor. Join and claim a base; buy squishies as they travel past and route them to a free slot.

Passive income

Every squishy in a base generates cash per second based on its value, accumulating at a collection point for the owner to claim.

Stealing system

Carry a squishy from another player's base back to your own. Carrying slows you, the owner is warned, and the theft only lands at home.

Base locking & defense

A lock button that drops barriers with a countdown and cooldown, plus defensive tools like a knockback hand, speed item, and bear trap.

Rarities & config

Common to Squishy Royalty, with mutations and spawn chances, built on a config system so new squishies and tiers drop in without rewrites.

Mystery boxes & rebirth

Dumpling-box gacha with weighted odds, and a rebirth loop granting income multipliers, more slots and floors, and longer base locks.

Monetization framework

Game passes, developer products, boosts, cosmetics and base skins, bundles, and gear, with clear purchase confirmations.

Onboarding & analytics

An interactive first-time tutorial and beginner quests, wired into a Roblox AnalyticsService onboarding funnel with custom events.

Server-authoritative stealing in multiplayer

The hardest and most interesting system is the stealing mechanic, because it has to be fair and exploit-proof in a live multiplayer server. A thief enters another player's unlocked base, picks up a squishy, and has to carry it all the way home. While carrying, their movement is slowed, the squishy shows on them, and the original owner gets a warning; other players can hit or stop them, and if the thief is knocked out, leaves, or resets, the squishy returns safely to its owner.

None of that is trusted to the client. Purchases, income, and every step of a theft are validated on the server, with explicit duplication prevention so a squishy can never exist in two bases at once, slot checks before a theft can complete, and DataStore saving for money, squishies, upgrades, and rebirths. Base locking (barriers, countdown, cooldown) and the defensive tools give owners a real counter-play.

Around that core, I built the systems that turn the loop into a game you keep coming back to: a config-driven rarity system from Common to Squishy Royalty, mystery-box gacha with weighted odds, a rebirth progression, a full monetization framework, an events and admin layer gated to authorized user IDs, and a first-time tutorial wired into an AnalyticsService onboarding funnel so player behaviour can actually be measured. It's all built to run cross-platform on phone, tablet, PC, and console.
