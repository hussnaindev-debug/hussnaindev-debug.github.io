The game

Steal a Viral Squishy is a Roblox collection and progression game. The core loop is about earning currency, spending it on upgrades, and rebirthing to push further, with quests guiding the player and a shop at the center of it all. I designed and built every system in the experience.

Around that loop sits a full stack of progression and retention features: a shop, a rebirth system, quests, and an index to track what the player has collected, all on a server-authoritative Roblox setup so progress and the in-game economy stay secure and persistent.

An upgrade economy that keeps players going

The heart of Steal a Viral Squishy is an upgrade economy: players earn currency, reinvest it through the shop, and rebirth to unlock bigger gains. Each system feeds the next, so there's always a clear next goal, which is what keeps a progression game engaging.

Layered on top are the retention systems, quests to guide players, an index to chase completion, and statistics to show progress. Everything runs server-authoritative so the economy and player data can't be tampered with from the client, which matters for a game built around earning and upgrading.
