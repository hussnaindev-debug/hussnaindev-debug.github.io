The game

Octopus Energy is a Roblox tycoon game. Players collect a variety of tycoons and build their base up around them, with a core loop that rewards expanding, earning, and reinvesting. I designed and built all of the systems in the experience, from the tycoon economy to the meta features that keep players engaged.

Around that core loop is a full stack of progression and reward systems: obbys to complete, collectables to track down, a pet system, daily rewards, a shop for spending earnings, and a leaderboard for competition. Everything runs on a server-authoritative Roblox setup so player progress and economy stay secure and persistent.

A full tycoon loop, server-authoritative

The heart of Octopus Energy is a tycoon economy: collect tycoons, earn from them, and reinvest to expand. I designed and built that core loop and then layered a full stack of progression and reward systems on top, obbys, collectables, a pet system, daily rewards, a shop, and a leaderboard, so there's always a next goal.

All of it runs on a server-authoritative Roblox setup, which keeps player progress and the in-game economy secure and persistent rather than trusting the client. Building every one of these systems myself meant they fit together into one coherent loop.
