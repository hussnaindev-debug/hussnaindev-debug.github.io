Overview

Octopus Energy is a Roblox tycoon game. Players collect a variety of tycoons and build their base up around them, with a core loop that rewards expanding, earning, and reinvesting. I designed and built all of the systems in the experience, from the tycoon economy to the meta features that keep players engaged.

Around that core loop is a full stack of progression and reward systems: obbys for players to complete, collectables to track down, a pet system, daily rewards to bring players back each day, a shop for spending earnings, and a leaderboard for competition. Everything is built on a server-authoritative Roblox setup so player progress and economy stay secure and persistent.
