Mouse Maze

Personal Project  ·  Solo Developer
WebGL Playable in Browser

A browser-based maze game where you guide a mouse through twisting corridors to reach the cheese, with controls that adapt automatically to whether you are on a PC or a phone.

Play on itch.io ↗ Get in Touch

Overview

Mouse Maze is a WebGL game built in Unity and published on itch.io, so it runs right in the browser with no download. You play as a mouse navigating a maze, working your way through the corridors to find the cheese waiting at the end.

The part I am most proud of is the control system. The game detects what device the player is on and enables the right scheme automatically: keyboard controls on desktop, and a responsive on-screen joystick on touch devices. That means the same WebGL build feels natural whether it is opened on a laptop or a phone, with no setup from the player.

The interface is built to feel alive. Menus, buttons, and panels are animated with DOTween, giving every interaction smooth motion and polish rather than hard cuts, which makes the whole experience feel responsive and finished.
