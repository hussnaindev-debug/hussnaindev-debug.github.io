The game

4 Reaction is a 2D local multiplayer party game made to be played with friends on a single device. As lead developer, I built it from scratch with no template or starter kit, owning the core systems and all 30 minigames.

The heart of the game is those 30 minigames, each a fast test of quick thinking and reaction. It has real personality too: answer correctly and it applauds you, slip up and it playfully taunts you, so every round stays lively.

Matches scale from one-on-one up to a four-player free-for-all, and a solo AI mode with adjustable difficulty means there's always someone to play against. An XP system rewards every match and unlocks new minigames over time.

What I built

Lead developer, from scratch

30 unique minigames

Thirty distinct challenges, each with its own rules, win conditions, and input style.

Modular architecture

Each minigame is self-contained: its own prefab, a ScriptableObject for settings, and its own script.

AI opponent

A solo mode against an AI with adjustable difficulty for when no friends are around.

XP and unlocks

Earn XP from matches and spend it to unlock more minigames over time.

IAP, analytics & ATT

In-app purchases for extra minigames, AppsFlyer analytics, and iOS ATT compliance.
