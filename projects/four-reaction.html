Overview

4 Reaction is a 2D local multiplayer party game made to be played with friends on a single device. As lead developer, I built the game from scratch with no template or starter kit, owning the core systems and all 30 minigames.

The heart of the game is its 30 unique minigames, each one a fast test of quick thinking and reaction. It has real personality too: answer correctly and it applauds you, slip up and it playfully taunts you, so every round stays lively and competitive.

Matches scale from one-on-one all the way to a four-player free-for-all, and a solo AI mode with adjustable difficulty means there is always someone to play against. An XP system rewards every match and lets players unlock new minigames over time, giving a satisfying sense of progression.

Under the hood, every minigame is fully self-contained, with its own prefab, a ScriptableObject that drives its settings, and its own dedicated script. This modular architecture made each minigame easy to build, tune, and expand on its own.

On the production side, I integrated in-app purchases so players can buy extra minigames, AppsFlyer for analytics, and full iOS ATT compliance for privacy-friendly tracking. The game reached 5,000+ installs on Google Play.
