4 Reaction

A 2D offline local-multiplayer party game packing 30 unique minigames — each with its own rules, win conditions, and input handling — wrapped in polished UI with IAP and analytics.

Google Play ↗ Get in Touch

Overview

4 Reaction is a couch-multiplayer party game where up to four players share one screen and battle across 30 distinct minigames. Each minigame is self-contained — its own ruleset, win condition, and input scheme — so the challenge was building a flexible framework that could host wildly different mechanics under one consistent flow.

On top of the gameplay, I integrated in-app purchases, AppsFlyer attribution, and Apple ATT compliance, and used DOTween to give the menus and transitions a satisfying, polished feel. The game reached 5,000+ installs on Google Play.

Key Features

🎮 30 Unique Minigames

Each with its own rules, win conditions, and input handling under one shared framework.

👥 Local Multiplayer

Offline same-screen play for up to four players — pure couch party fun.

✨ DOTween Polish

Smooth, animated UI transitions and feedback throughout the menus and rounds.

💰 In-App Purchases

IAP integration for monetization.

📊 AppsFlyer Analytics

Attribution and event tracking for growth insights.

🔒 iOS ATT Compliance

Privacy-compliant App Tracking Transparency flows.
