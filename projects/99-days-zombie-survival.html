The game

99 Days Zombie Survival is a story-driven Roblox title built in Roblox Studio with Luau. As a core developer on the project, I contributed across gameplay systems, UI, and performance, taking the experience from early build to a live game that reached 155,800+ visits and 1,200+ favorites within its first four months.

The game blends narrative delivery with action survival: scripted cutscenes drive the story between waves, while a modular weapons system and gacha rolls give players long-term progression goals. A heavy focus on optimization keeps it smooth on mobile and low-end Roblox clients.

Narrative meets survival

The design challenge on 99 Days Zombie Survival was blending a story-driven experience with action survival on Roblox. Scripted cutscenes built in Moon Animator drive the narrative between waves, and integrating them cleanly into the gameplay flow, so they support the action instead of interrupting it, was a core part of my work.

On the progression side, a modular weapons system handles combat logic, animations, and player interaction across many weapon types, while a gacha system with rarity tiers gives players long-term collection goals. Backing it all is a heavy optimization pass so the experience stays smooth on mobile and low-end clients, which matters for reach on Roblox. Together these helped the game reach 155,800+ visits and 1,200+ favorites in its first four months.
