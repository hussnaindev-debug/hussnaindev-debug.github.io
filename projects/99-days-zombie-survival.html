99 Days Zombie Survival

ZhenStudio – 26  ·  Roblox Game Developer

A narrative-driven Roblox survival experience where players endure 99 days against the undead — built with cinematic cutscenes, a modular weapons system, gacha mechanics, and performance tuned for low-end mobile devices.

Play on Roblox ↗ Get in Touch

Overview

99 Days Zombie Survival is a story-driven Roblox title built in Roblox Studio with Luau. As a core developer on the project, I contributed across gameplay systems, UI, and performance — taking the experience from early build to a live game that reached 155,800+ visits and 1,200+ favorites within its first four months of launch.

The game blends narrative delivery with action survival: scripted cutscenes drive the story between waves, while a modular weapons system and gacha rolls give players long-term progression goals. A heavy focus on optimization keeps it smooth on mobile and low-end Roblox clients.

Key Features

🎬 Cinematic Cutscenes

Scripted in-game cutscenes built with Moon Animator, integrated seamlessly into the gameplay flow to deliver the story.

🔫 Modular Weapons System

Scalable weapons framework handling combat logic, animations, and player interaction across many weapon types.

🎰 Gacha System

Randomized reward rolls with rarity tiers, driving collection and re-engagement.

🎒 Backpack & UI

Inventory UI, tutorial onboarding flows, and dynamic toast messaging for clean player feedback.

⚡ Mobile Optimization

Performance tuning to keep gameplay smooth on low-end and mobile-supported Roblox devices.
