About

A bit about me.

I'm a game developer from Pakistan working across Unity/C# and Roblox/Luau. I've shipped titles with real players on Google Play, the App Store, and Roblox, and I'm most at home in the guts of a game: player controls, physics, AI, and the meta systems, shops, garages, progression, leaderboards, that keep people coming back.

On Roblox I build narrative-driven experiences with cutscenes, modular weapons, and gacha systems, tuned to run well on low-end devices. I work comfortably in remote agile teams alongside designers, artists, and QA using Git and Jira, and I care about actually shipping, not just prototyping.
